# Sources Used

**ALL** sources used are from **publicly available data**. ***There is no sensitive student information contained in the data derived from these sources.***

## Florida Education

1. **[Florida Education Finance Program (FEFP)](https://www.fldoe.org/finance/fl-edu-finance-program-fefp/)**: The Office of Funding and Financial Reporting (OFFR) has two primary functions: (1) Calculation and distribution of funds provided through the Florida Education Finance Program (FEFP) and state categorical aid programs such as pupil transportation; and (2) Financial reporting of school districts. [Direct link to FDOE's FEFP FTE Calculation data](https://www.fldoe.org/finance/fl-edu-finance-program-fefp/fl-edu-finance-program-fefp-calculatio.stml)

2. **[Student Enrollment (FTE), History](https://www.fldoe.org/finance/fl-edu-finance-program-fefp/fte-info/student-enrollment.stml)**:
These data are archival compilations of the FEFP unweighted FTE calculation data in Excel format.

3. **[Capital Outlay FTE (COFTE)](https://www.fldoe.org/finance/fl-edu-finance-program-fefp/capital-outlay-fte-cofte.stml)**:
The COFTE data are compiled from Excel datasets.

4. **[Membership by School by Grade: Enrollment](https://www.fldoe.org/accountability/data-sys/edu-info-accountability-services/pk-12-public-school-data-pubs-reports/students.stml)**: Provides student membership count datasets by school and by grade for the current school year. Previous year datasets are available at: [Archived Student Membership: Membership by Grade (PK-12)](https://www.fldoe.org/accountability/data-sys/edu-info-accountability-services/pk-12-public-school-data-pubs-reports/archive.stml).

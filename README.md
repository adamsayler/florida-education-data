# Florida Education Data

A repository of transformed  and combined Florida Department of Education datasets

## Purpose

Each year school district finance officers in Florida must create budgets in accordance with the **Florida Education Finance Programs (FEFP)** based upon predicted calculations provided by the **Florida Department of Education (FDOE)**. This data must be extracted and transformed into a useful format for school district staff. This project seems to assist this process by providing the FDOE datasets as compiled and transformed datasets the represents multiple years and multiple calculation periods. Other data are also formatted and compiled here as are relevant to the budgeting process.
